# Citation-prediction evidence

This offline prototype records testable hypotheses rather than claiming live-web causal proof. CG-002 predicts that `raw_html_contains_price` makes citation more likely; the local SPA contrast is 1/1 rendered-only versus 1/1 static evidence sites. Until a cited-vs-uncited public sample is collected, this signal is exploratory outside its rendering gate and cannot exceed medium without CG-002's four conditions.

Structured entity markup, stable facts, and explicit source context are likewise rationale for checks, not evidence of a specific search engine ranking behavior. The report retains `insufficient_evidence` whenever cross-web corroboration is disabled.
